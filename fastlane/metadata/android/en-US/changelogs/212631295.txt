What's new in this release:

- Cloud backups now work on Android. Point Bramble at storage you already have, an S3-compatible bucket such as Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2 or Wasabi, or a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, and it keeps a copy of your vault there, still sealed with your master password so the provider cannot read it. A backup happens when you unlock Bramble or come back to it after the interval you chose has passed. Back up now does it straight away.
- Leaving Bramble during a backup no longer cuts it off. It keeps running until the upload is done, and you may see a notification saying so. If a backup could not be confirmed, Settings tells you, and it runs again the next time you open Bramble.
- Two more email alias services: DuckDuckGo Email Protection and Firefox Relay. DuckDuckGo has no page for API keys, so Settings explains where to find the one it uses.
- Switching to a different alias service now clears the API key box. Before, a key typed for one service stayed there, and checking it would send it to the service you had just switched to.
- Pairing two devices no longer fails while you are still confirming it. It could time out partway and report that the other device had rejected the pairing, when nobody had.
- Searching and selecting in a large vault is smoother. The list no longer redraws every item on each keystroke.
- In German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese, a few instructions were cut off mid-sentence, including the ones for pairing with another device. They are complete now.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
