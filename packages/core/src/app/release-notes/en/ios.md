Bramble now blocks screenshots and screen recordings, and can sync through a mailbox.

- Bramble now keeps your vault out of screenshots, screen recordings and screen mirroring. To take a screenshot, choose Allow for 10 minutes under Block screenshots in Settings → General.
- Sync through a mailbox when your devices are never online together: sign in with a Bramble Mailbox account number, or use your own server, in Settings → Sync → Mailbox.
- SSH keys can list the hosts they're for, and the desktop app can now use them in the terminal.
- Exporting to KeePass keeps a login's extra websites where KeePassXC and Keepass2Android read them.
- Deleting one of two vaults from the list of vaults now opens the one that's left.
- Devices that unlock just after the hour now find each other right away.
